Have you ever bought a music CD and wanted to put the songs on your computer or phone, but you did not know how? Today I will show you how to get music files off a CD. People call this **"Rip"**. We will do it on **Windows 11**, and we do not need to find or install any extra software!

### What is Rip?

**Rip** means we copy the audio from a music CD into digital files on our computer. We get music files like `.mp3`, `.wma`, `.flac`, or other formats. Then it is easy to play them on many devices.

### Steps to Rip on Windows 11

It is very easy. Let's follow the steps one by one.

#### 1. Put in the CD

The first step is to put your music CD into the CD/DVD drive of your computer.

But wait... most new computers do not have a CD slot anymore, right? Don't worry. You can buy an **External CD/DVD Drive** and plug it in. It is not expensive at all.

#### 2. Open the Media Player app

Next, search for **"Media Player"** and open it. This is the new media player that comes with **Windows 11**.

![Search for the Media Player app](/blog/how-to-rip-music-cd-on-windows-11/2.png)

#### 3. Go to the Audio CD tab

When the app opens, look at the menu on the left. Find the name of your CD. It may say **"Audio CD"**, or it may show the album name. Click it. You will see all the songs on the disc.

At this point, the song info, like the song name and the artist name, may show up. Or you may only see **"Track 1"**, **"Track 2"**. It depends on whether the app's database knows this album.

![List of songs on the CD](/blog/how-to-rip-music-cd-on-windows-11/3.png)

#### 4. Set up the Rip settings

Before we click Rip, I suggest you check the settings first. Click the **"Rip settings"** menu.

![The Rip settings menu button](/blog/how-to-rip-music-cd-on-windows-11/4.png)

Here you can choose the **Format** (the file type) and the **Audio quality** (or **Bit rate**) that you want.

- **Format**: I suggest `MP3`. It is the most popular format, and every device can play it. If you want the best sound quality with no compression, you can choose `FLAC (lossless)`.
- **Audio quality (Bit rate)**: A higher number gives better sound, but the file gets bigger too. For `MP3`, I suggest `320 kbps`. That is already very good quality. (Some formats, like `FLAC`, do not let you choose the **Bit rate**.)

When you finish, click **"Save"**.

![The Rip settings page](/blog/how-to-rip-music-cd-on-windows-11/5.png)

#### 5. Click Rip CD

When you are ready, just click the **"Rip CD"** button!

![The Rip CD button](/blog/how-to-rip-music-cd-on-windows-11/6.png)

#### 6. (Optional) Edit the song info before you Rip

Maybe the app does not show the song info (you only see **Track 1**, **Track 2**...), and you want to add the info yourself before you Rip. You can do that. Click **"Edit info"**.

![The Edit info button](/blog/how-to-rip-music-cd-on-windows-11/7.png)

Then fill in the details. You can add the album name, the artist name, the genre, the release year, each song name, and even the album cover. When you finish, click **"Rip"**.

![The Edit info page](/blog/how-to-rip-music-cd-on-windows-11/8.png)

Personally, I often just Rip first with no song info. I fix the info later with a program called **[Mp3tag](https://www.mp3tag.de/en/)**. It is a free program, and it is very good at managing the tags of music files. It is much easier!

#### 7. Wait until it finishes

Now we just wait. The app will Rip the songs from the disc one by one. The status of the song that is being ripped shows as **_n_% ripped**. When it finishes, the status changes to **"Rip complete"**.

![The status of the song Rip](/blog/how-to-rip-music-cd-on-windows-11/9.png)

#### 8. Find your music files

When all the songs are done, your music files are saved in the **`Music`** folder. Usually the app makes a folder with the artist name, and inside it there is a folder with the album name.

If you cannot find them, try this path: **`C:\Users\<YourUser>\Music`** (change `<YourUser>` to your own user name).

![The Music folder with an artist folder](/blog/how-to-rip-music-cd-on-windows-11/10.png)

![The album folder inside the artist folder](/blog/how-to-rip-music-cd-on-windows-11/11.png)

![The music files after the Rip is done](/blog/how-to-rip-music-cd-on-windows-11/12.png)

That is all! Now we have the music files from the CD on our computer. You can put them on your phone or a portable music player right away.
